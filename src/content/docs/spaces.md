title: Spaces, Orbit, and categories

A space is one set of links, feeds, and apps. The examples people start from are along the lines of Dev Matrix, Homelab & Infra, Live Pulse, and Personal. Switch with `Tab` and `Shift+Tab`, or click the space in the top bar. Create, rename, and delete spaces from that bar.

## Quick Access Orbit

Each space has six slots. Pin a web link, an Omarchy action, a local application, or a shell command, then launch it with `1` through `6` while search is empty. The Note space does not show this strip. A pin there keeps a [note](/notes/) at the top of the list.

Moving an item to another space clears its orbit pin.

## Categories

Columns are color-coded categories. Colors follow the active Omarchy theme: `Color`, `Style`, `Border`, and the semantic ANSI colors in `colors.toml` (`blue`, `cyan`, `green`, `magenta`, `yellow`, `orange`, `red`). The filter badges under the search field stay on one row and wrap when there are many categories.

A column is a static bookmark list, an RSS or Atom feed, or a GitHub repository. Feed setup is on [Live columns](/live-columns/).

## Order and moving

Hover a static bookmark and use ▲ / ▼ to reorder it inside its category. The arrows hide while you are searching or while the Favorites filter is on.

**Move to…** in the context menu, or the space and category fields in the edit dialog, send an item to another space. Right-click or `Alt+M` opens the menu. `Alt+E` opens the editor.
